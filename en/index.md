# PitStop | Web Design, Meta Ads & Branding in Egypt

PitStop is a digital team in Egypt. We build websites, custom systems, branding, Meta ads and short-form video that put local brands in front of the customers already looking for them — on time, with nothing hidden.

## Our Services

1. **Websites & Custom Systems** — Mobile-friendly websites and custom systems like booking and order management, built around how your business actually works: landing pages, multi-page websites, custom systems.
2. **Design & Visual Identity** — Logo, colors and type that make your brand recognizable everywhere, from social media to print: visual identity & logo, social media design, print design.
3. **Meta Ads** — Facebook and Instagram campaigns that reach people interested in what you sell, with results we review with you: Facebook & Instagram ad management.
4. **Video & Reels** — Short videos for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok that introduce your brand in seconds: short-form videos & reels.

## How We Work (The Pit Stop)

A pit stop is where a car gets fixed fast and sent back out. Your project moves through the same four stages, from first diagnosis to launch.

1. **Diagnose** — A clear read on your brand: what works, what's missing, where the opening is.
2. **Plan** — You know the scope, the direction, and what success looks like before work starts.
3. **Build** — You see the work at every checkpoint, not a black box.
4. **Launch** — The finished project handed over, with support through go-live.
