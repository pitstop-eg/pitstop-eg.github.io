# PitStop

The marketing site of PitStop, a three-person digital solutions agency. It presents the agency to small and medium local brands and routes them to one contact action. The racing / pit-stop metaphor is brand language, not a feature of the product.

## Language

**PitStop**:
The agency itself. Written as one word, no space, always capital P and S.
_Avoid_: Pit Stop (for the company), PIT STOP (outside the logo lockup)

**Brand**:
A client business that PitStop works for, treated as an identity to be positioned and equipped rather than as a company to be serviced.
_Avoid_: Client, customer, account

**Pit Stop**:
PitStop's four-stage process for a project — Diagnose, Plan, Build, Launch — and the only section of the site that uses the racing metaphor literally.
_Avoid_: Workflow, methodology, how we work (as a separate section)

**Lane**:
One of the four service categories on the site — Web & Systems, Design, Ads, Video. Lanes carry unequal weight; Web & Systems is the deepest.
_Avoid_: Service, offering, department, pillar

**Primary action**:
The single contact route the site pushes every visitor toward. It starts as the project brief form and is intended to become WhatsApp once a business number exists.
_Avoid_: CTA, conversion, lead form
