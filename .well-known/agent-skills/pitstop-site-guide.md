# PitStop Site Guide

PitStop is a digital-presence agency for small and medium local brands in Egypt. Site: https://pitstop-eg.github.io (Arabic) / https://pitstop-eg.github.io/en/ (English).

## Services

1. **Websites & Custom Systems** — mobile-friendly websites, landing pages, custom systems (booking, order management).
2. **Design & Visual Identity** — logo, brand identity, social media and print design.
3. **Meta Ads** — Facebook and Instagram campaign management.
4. **Video & Reels** — short-form video for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok.

## Process

Four stages: Diagnose → Plan → Build → Launch. Clients see the work at every checkpoint.
